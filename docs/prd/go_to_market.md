# Starting Position & Go-to-Market

## The Launch Base

VVS Members is not launching into a vacuum. VVS Lagos is an established creative community with institutional backing (Sterling Bank, MTN, British Council, Lagos State Government) and **over 3,000 existing signed-up members** — designers, photographers, stylists, developers, musicians, and other creative professionals active in Lagos.

This existing community is the launch base. The go-to-market strategy flows directly from it.

## Supply-Side Strategy

**Seed supply in two high-demand categories.** Design (brand identity, graphic design, UI/UX) and photography/videography will be the first categories with enough provider density and client demand to generate real transactions. These are the two categories most frequently requested through VVS Lagos's existing community channels.

**Convert existing community.** The first batch of invite codes will be distributed to the most active VVS Lagos members — those who have attended multiple events, contributed to community projects, or have established reputations within the network. Target: convert 20–25% of the existing 3,000+ base within the first 3 months (600–750 members), with at least 200 listing active services.

**Expand through referral chains.** Once the initial marketplace has enough liquidity in the seed categories, open up invite allocations for Verified and Pro members to pull in their own professional networks — extending beyond the VVS Lagos core into the broader Lagos creative economy and eventually other Nigerian cities.

## Demand-Side Strategy

Creative communities skew heavily toward providers — people offering services outnumber people hiring. If 60–70% of the VVS Lagos base are providers, the demand bottleneck becomes the binding constraint on GMV.

To address this, the platform must actively pull in **buyers who are not currently in the VVS Lagos creative community**:

- **Startup founders and SME operators** who regularly hire designers, photographers, and content creators but aren't part of creative circles. These are reached through existing members' professional networks (a designer invites the founder she works for).
- **Marketing and communications leads at mid-size Lagos companies** who manage freelancer budgets. Targeted outreach through VVS Lagos's institutional partners.
- **Event organisers, fashion brands, and restaurant owners** who are high-frequency buyers of creative services. Many already interact with VVS Lagos members informally.

The invite system is the primary demand-side acquisition channel: providers are encouraged to invite their best clients, not just other creatives. Invite allocation messaging should prompt: *"Who do you wish was hiring on this platform?"*

## Category Expansion Roadmap

Design and photography first → content writing and copywriting → software development and tech consulting → event planning and production → fashion and styling. Each new category is activated only when there's demonstrated demand from the existing member base (measured by search queries with no results and direct member requests).
