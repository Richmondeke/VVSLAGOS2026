# Goals & Success Metrics

## North Star Metric

**Gross Merchandise Value (GMV)** — the total value of transactions completed through escrow. This is the single number that tells us whether VVS Members is generating real economic activity, not just signups and profiles.

**GMV target (first 6 months post-launch):** ₦50M+ in completed escrow transactions.

There are multiple paths to this target, reducing fragility:

| Path | Transactions | Avg Order Value | GMV |
|------|-------------|----------------|-----|
| **High volume** (many smaller engagements) | 400 | ₦130K | ₦52M |
| **Balanced** (baseline expectation) | 300 | ₦175K | ₦52.5M |
| **High value** (fewer, larger projects) | 220 | ₦230K | ₦50.6M |

The target is achievable through different mixes of transaction volume and order size — the business does not depend on hitting every metric perfectly.

## Supporting Metrics

| Metric | Target (6 months) | Why It Matters |
|--------|-------------------|----------------|
| **Completed escrow transactions** | 250–400 | Volume of real commercial activity |
| **Average order value** | ₦130K–230K (target midpoint: ₦175K) | Ensures the platform attracts meaningful professional engagements, not micro-tasks |
| **Registered members** | 800–1,000 | Community growth (conservative given 3,000+ existing VVS Lagos base) |
| **Members with active listings** | 200+ | Supply-side health — enough providers to give clients real choice |
| **Provider-to-client ratio** | Tracked from day one; target ≤ 3:1 | If this skews beyond 3:1, demand-side acquisition must accelerate |
| **Provider retention** (listed a service AND completed ≥1 transaction in the last 90 days) | 50%+ | Providers who are earning stay; providers who aren't, leave |
| **Client retention** (funded ≥1 order in the last 90 days) | 30%+ | Clients may hire quarterly, not monthly — a lower bar than provider retention is realistic |
| **Dispute rate** | < 5% of funded orders | Trust and quality signal |
| **Referral conversion** | 30%+ of generated invites redeemed | Community is actively pulling people in |
| **Funnel conversion** (browse → order → fund → complete) | Tracked from day one; no target yet — baseline measurement | Identifies where drop-off happens |

## Business Goals

| Goal | How We Measure It |
|------|-------------------|
| **G1 — Trusted marketplace** | GMV growth, dispute rate, average reputation score |
| **G2 — Transaction volume** | Completed orders, repeat transaction rate |
| **G3 — Community quality** | Profile completeness, provider-to-client ratio, moderation action rate |
| **G4 — Revenue generation** | Platform fee revenue (see Section 7.4 for fee structure) |
