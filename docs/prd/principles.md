# Product Principles

These principles guide every product decision. When in doubt, they resolve trade-offs.

1. **Transactions over engagement.** The platform exists to facilitate paid professional work, not content consumption. Every feature should drive or support real economic activity.
2. **Reputation reflects real economic activity.** No score, badge, or ranking should be achievable without money changing hands for real work.
3. **Trust over growth.** A smaller, high-trust network is more valuable than a large, low-trust one. We will sacrifice growth rate to maintain community quality.
4. **Simplicity over feature breadth.** Ship fewer things well. Complexity is added only when validated by observed user behaviour, not anticipated need.
